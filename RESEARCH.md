### Eligibility (Official Rules §3) — SOLO + INDIA VERIFIED

- Open to **individuals who are at least the age of majority where they
  reside** at time of entry. User is 18+ (confirmed 4 Nov 2026) and resident
  in India, where the age of majority is 18. ✅
- Open to teams of eligible individuals and to organisations. **No team-size
  minimum — solo entry is explicitly allowed.** ✅
- NOT open to residents of: **Brazil, Quebec, Russia, Belarus, Crimea, Cuba,
  Iran, North Korea, Syria, Sudan, Myanmar**, and any other country designated
  by the US Treasury OFAC. **India is NOT on the excluded list.** ✅
- Also excluded: people on US Commerce Denied Persons / Treasury SDN lists,
  and employees/agents of Atlassian/Devpost and their immediate families.
- One entrant may submit **more than one submission**, each substantially
  different; one entrant may also join a team AND enter individually.
- "NO PURCHASE OR PAYMENT NECESSARY TO ENTER OR WIN." Free-only rule is
  compatible. A free Atlassian Cloud Developer License is the required route
  (Rules §4).

### What to build (Official Rules §4)

- A **Forge app** integrating **at least one** of: Jira, Confluence, Jira
  Service Management, Bitbucket, **Teamwork Graph**, Rovo.
- Categories (one per submission):
  1. **Apps for Software Teams** — smarter code reviews, deployment awareness,
     issue triage that understands what matters. ← SecretGate enters here.
  2. Apps for Business Teams
  3. Apps for Specialized Industries (healthcare, finance, manufacturing,
     automotive, …)
- **New & existing rule:** projects must be newly created (not published
  before the Submission Period) OR, if they existed before, must have been
  **updated or rebuilt on Forge after 27 Oct 2026** with a Forge manifest and
  at least one Forge module. SecretGate is newly created on Forge — clean.
- The project must be installable in a judge's test environment **via an
  installation link**, run consistently, and function as depicted in the video.

### What to submit (Official Rules §4)

1. Installation link + full instructions for the judges' environment.
2. **Demo video < 5 minutes** (judges not required to watch beyond 3 min),
   **public on YouTube**, showing the project functioning.
3. Category + bonus prizes selected, with any bonus write-up.
4. Text description + explanation of how the project meets the Forge /
   Atlassian-product requirement.
5. All materials in English.

### Judging (Official Rules §6)

- Stage One: pass/fail viability (fits theme, uses required APIs/SDKs).
- Stage Two: five criteria, **20% each** — Technological Implementation,
  Design, Potential Impact, Innovation/Idea, Presentation. Tie-breaks go
  criterion by criterion in that order.
